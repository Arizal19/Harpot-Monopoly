4D Wizard House — standalone demo

Realistic procedural houses (Three.js r128, no image assets): stone cottage (level 1) and two-story timber house (level 2), with chimney smoke, flickering windows, swaying banner, breathing rune ring and a rise-and-pop entrance.

The house holder sits at z = 20, the tile top face at z ~= -2 + 4U, pawns at z = 40 + k. Higher z draws later, so the house sits above the tile artwork and behind the pawns. The house is posed like the tile slabs (pitch + yaw), so it leans toward the camera. Its base disc and rune ring stay under ~42 units against a ~59 unit tile, so the artwork frames the house on all sides.
